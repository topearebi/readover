Your notes

Organising your notes
  • Types are folders. Each top-level folder is a type (Journal, Essays, Highlights…), and its subfolders belong to it. Notes at the top level are Unsorted. No folders at all works too.
  • New notes are named by date and title, e.g. 2026-10-11-on-habits.md, or the first words when there's no title. Readover adds a little frontmatter (title, date, tags) that any Markdown app can read.
  • Status: a note is in progress until you mark it done (status: done). Done notes rest: they don't wilt.
  • Highlights you save from books, notes and clips go to the folder above.
  • Archive holds pruned notes. It isn't a type.
  • Renaming: rename or move notes in Readover (a note's ⋯ menu) to keep links working: links to it are updated in the same commit. Renamed elsewhere, links by the old file name stop working.
How the garden works
  • Stages: Seed (no thoughts or links yet), Sprout (a thought or a link), Sapling (2+ thoughts and 2+ links), Tree (5+ links).
  • A thought is your own words in a note, plus each dated entry under ## Thoughts. Links count both ways.
  • Health comes from when the note last changed: healthy, then thirsty, then wilting. Seeds wilt after a month, sprouts after 3 months, saplings after a year, trees after 2 years.
  • The thirstier a note, the more often it comes up. Water adds a thought, Graft adds a link, Prune moves it to Archive (and Replant brings it back).
  • Moving or renaming notes outside Readover can restart their clock.

Clip from anywhere

A clip is a quote, where it’s from, and your thought. It’s saved like any new note.

On a computer

Drag this to your bookmarks bar. Select text on a page, then click it.

Clip to Readover

On Android

Add Readover to your home screen. Then select text in any app and choose Share → Readover.

On iPhone or iPad

Make a Shortcut once, in the Shortcuts app:

  1. New shortcut. In its settings (ⓘ), turn on Show in Share Sheet and accept URLs and Safari web pages.
  2. Add Get Clipboard, then URL Encode it.
  3. Add URL Encode on the Shortcut Input (the page).
  4. Add Open URLs with: , putting the encoded clipboard after text= and the encoded page after url=.
  5. Name it Readover.

To clip: select text, tap Copy, then Share → Readover.

Anywhere: copy a passage, then tap the pen and Paste a quote.

Keep your notes safe

Right now your notes live only in this browser. If its data is cleared, they're gone. On GitHub they become plain Markdown files in a private repository only you can see. GitHub is free, and your favourites and reading positions will follow you to any device.

  1. Create a private repository

    It's filled in for you: just press Create repository. Already have one for notes? Skip this.

    Create it on GitHub

  2. Create a token for Readover

    Also filled in. Under Repository access, choose Only select repositories and pick your notes repository. Then press Generate token and copy it.

    Create a token

  3. Connect

Leave it empty to keep your types (folders) as they are.

Your token stays in this browser. Readover only talks to GitHub.
